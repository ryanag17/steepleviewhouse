Privacy Policy & Terms and Conditions

1. Introduction

We at Steepleview House value your privacy and ensure that your personal data is handled responsibly. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or book a stay with us. This policy complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other relevant laws in the European Union.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Processing booking inquiries and bookings.
  • Communicating with you regarding your booking or inquiries.
  • Improving our services and website functionality.
  • Complying with legal requirements, such as tax obligations.

4. Personal Data We Collect

We may collect the following personal data from you:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Booking details (dates, room selection)
  • Payment information (when booking directly)
  • Other information necessary for completing your stay

5. Legal Basis for Data Processing

The legal basis for processing your personal data under the GDPR is as follows:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR): For instance, when you fill out the booking inquiry form.
  • Contractual necessity (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): Processing your data to fulfill your booking or reservation.
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR): We are obligated to provide your information to local tax authorities if required.
  • Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): For instance, for improving services or security measures.

6. Sharing Your Data

We do not share your personal data with third parties except in the following cases:

  • With local tax authorities as required by German law.
  • When necessary for the fulfillment of a contract (e.g., payment processing or booking fulfillment).
  • When we have your explicit consent to share data.

7. Data Storage and Deletion

Your personal data will be stored securely and for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. Once your data is no longer required (such as after passing information to tax authorities or after the completion of your stay), it will be deleted or anonymized according to the applicable data retention regulations.

8. Your Rights

As per the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access: You have the right to request access to your personal data.
  • Right to rectification: You have the right to request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure: You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data in certain situations.
  • Right to restriction of processing: You have the right to request the restriction of processing in certain situations.
  • Right to object: You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing purposes.
  • Right to data portability: You have the right to request the transfer of your data to another service provider in certain circumstances.

If you wish to exercise any of your rights, please contact us via the information provided above.

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure the security of your personal data and protect it from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. This includes using encryption and secure communication protocols such as SSL (Secure Sockets Layer).

10. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your user experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us improve site navigation and functionality. You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies, but some website features may not work as intended.
