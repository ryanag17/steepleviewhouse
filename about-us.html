About Steepleview House

Our journey, our special village, and highlights in our area of Germany.

Our Story

In 2019, we, Melanie and Achim Geiler, bought Steepleview House and began extensive renovations. We opened our first two apartments by the end of the year, and later renovated the downstairs to introduce two new guest rooms and a cozy breakfast room. Our vision was to create a welcoming home for travelers in the heart of the Black Forest.

Bad Peterstal-Griesbach

Bad Peterstal-Griesbach is a charming village nestled within the scenic Black Forest. Known for its serene nature trails, cozy atmosphere, and authentic local culture, this village offers a perfect blend of relaxation and adventure for our guests.

Special Times of the Year

Fasching

Experience the colorful and lively Fasching festival, a time of celebration and community. Enjoy traditional parades, costumes, and dances.

Festivals

From music to local culture, our festivals showcase the best of Bad Peterstal-Griesbach. Join us for unforgettable experiences throughout the year.

Christmas Markets

The Christmas markets here are enchanting, filled with festive lights, handmade gifts, and the warmth of holiday cheer.
